# iPhone Microscope

A native Mac workbench viewer with a paired iPhone camera companion for inspecting, capturing, and measuring small objects.

Choose Continuity Camera, a USB camera, or the paired iPhone companion. The companion exposes supported rear lenses, camera zoom, focus, exposure and torch controls. The Mac viewer adds stabilization, image adjustments, reference frames, still/video capture and calibrated line measurements. Digital zoom enlarges pixels; it is not optical magnification.

The iPhone companion is listed in private TestFlight as **Bench Lens: Microscope Camera**, version 1.0 (2). It requires the separate Mac viewer, which currently uses local development distribution. No public App Store download or public beta invitation is advertised. Pairing requires a code, encrypted local transport, Camera and Local Network permission, and both apps in the foreground.

Physical testing on October 30 delivered about 59 FPS at 1920 × 1440 over Continuity Camera. The companion route delivered about 3 FPS at 1080p and 0.75 FPS at 4K in the latest test; it needs throughput work before smooth live soldering use. Twenty software tests and short recording/controls checks pass. These measurements are device-specific; latency, long-session reliability and certified measurement accuracy are not established.

The Ender 3 V2 overhead stand is a separate printable prototype. Its geometry has been checked, but physical fit, load and clearance are unverified. This is an inspection tool, not a certified measurement instrument.

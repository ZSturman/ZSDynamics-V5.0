# Camera Monitor

A native camera dashboard for iPhone, iPad, and Mac, with direct providers and optional Home Services connections.

Connect supported RTSP, HLS, MJPEG, ONVIF, go2rtc, Frigate, or Home Services sources. Organize cameras, inspect snapshots and available recordings, and see connection status. Provider capabilities vary; live viewing does not imply recording support.

Version 0.1.0 (4) refreshes the private TestFlight beta with current Connected Apps support. No public App Store download or open beta invitation is advertised. Camera streams and recording controls require their own provider configuration and permissions; approving a status summary does not authorize stream access.

A local backend is optional. Hardware compatibility, network conditions, and extended camera sessions remain separate acceptance checks.

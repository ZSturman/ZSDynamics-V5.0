# Trackables

A personal recorder for counts, choices, amounts, and duration, with widgets and an offline-capable Watch companion.

Create a Trackable, record in a tap, or add optional typed fields and reusable custom answers. Review History, inspect descriptive charts, and export CSV or JSON. There are no goals, streaks, or behavioral scores.

The iPhone/iPad release is available on the App Store. The current 1.0.1 (9) beta improves widget controls and Watch entry and fixes a background refresh crash. The native Mac edition is distributed separately through private TestFlight. Watch capture can queue records offline; cross-device synchronization and installed-device acceptance have their own limits.

Optional Connected Apps summary sharing requires source-owned approval. It does not grant other apps general access to your history.
